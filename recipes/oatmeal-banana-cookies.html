Oatmeal Banana Cookies

Here's a simple three ingredient recipe for delicious oatmeal banana cookies!

Ingredients:

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon

Optional Mix-ins:

  • chocolate chips
  • raisins or dried cranberries
  • chopped walnuts or pecans
  • shredded coconut
  • vanilla extract

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  3. Mash the bananas in a large bowl until smooth.
  4. Stir in the oats and cinnamon.
  5. Fold in any optional mix-ins.
  6. Drop rounded tablespoons of batter onto ungreased baking sheets.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until golden brown.

For a More Cookie-Like Texture:

Add:

  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter or almond butter
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup or honey
  • 1/4 cup chocolate chips

These cookies won't be cripsy like traditional cookies - they're soft, chewy, and more like a healthy snack or breakfast cookie.
